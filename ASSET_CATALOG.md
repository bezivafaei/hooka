## Production mapping

- `public/images/hero` — hero-specific optimized crop source.
- `public/images/stage` — temporary photograph for the future GLB/WebGL section.
- `public/images/products/{product}` — one full shot and one detail shot per current menu item.
- `public/images/editorial` — supporting collection imagery.
- `public/og.png` — bespoke social preview card.

All production JPEGs are resized to a maximum edge of 1600–2400 px and compressed to 82–84% quality. Full-resolution originals remain recoverable in `../Brand Assets`.
